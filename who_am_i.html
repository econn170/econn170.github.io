about

my name's ellie. i'm an mlis candidate at pratt institute and a moma library fellow, graduating in may 2026. my work sits at the intersection of digital archives, knowledge organization, and participatory art education — and i'm drawn to questions about how the systems we build for information shape what's possible to know and who gets to know it.

before library school, i studied user experience research and design at the university of michigan, and spent two years teaching english in spain. i've worked in startup, corporate, and cultural heritage contexts — from workit health and viacomcbs to the media burn archive and moma — and i bring that range with me into everything i do.

my creative practice includes a personal collage diary, a digital zine (tongue of the body: notes on desire), and machine divinity, a research-creation project involving fine-tuned language models trained on eschatological language from 4chan. i'm also developing odd collage, a digital collage platform for kids.

my work is guided by three values:

  1. empathy i believe we need to first understand people and their experiences before we can create systems for them.
  2. curiosity i have a genuine interest in why the world looks the way it does — and am always open to how it could look different.
  3. creativity i cherish the ways we express ourselves beyond words or traditional methods. artistic expression is life affirming.
